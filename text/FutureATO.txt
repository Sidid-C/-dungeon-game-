You creek open the wooden door and go into the basement. 
It is so dark you can’t see your hands in front of you, so you flip on your phone’s flashlight 
as you enter. There are many trophies and memorabilia of the abandoned fraternity. 
The underground is musty and wet and you can feel the air in your nostrils as you breathe. 
You see a book shelf and pull at the most recent year book from 1983. The book doesn’t come loose, 
but the entire bookshelf does. As you pull at the shelf, decades of dust kick up into the air,
but as the dust clears a tunnel is revealed. The tunnel resembles a mine shaft with wooden planks 
holding up the dirt and rock periodically. You travel through the tunnel.
Suddenly there are strobe lights and people dancing shoulder to shoulder in a basement that looks 
to be the one you just came from. Their hair is high and their clothes are neon. You run up the 
basement stairs.
