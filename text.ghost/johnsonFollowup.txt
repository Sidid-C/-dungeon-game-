The common room has patterned yellow couches and chairs with purple throw pillows 
decorating the cushions and clashing with the custard colored walls. There is an outdated 
kitchenette stuffed in the back corner and a coffee table in the center of the furniture. 
On the coffee table is a vase of dying daisies. The room is fully carpeted and has a 
fireplace on the other end. Above the fireplace, a hunting rifle is displayed.
