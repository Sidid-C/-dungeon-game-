In the night, you can’t make out a lot of the greenery, but still you follow the stepping stones deeper 
into Abbos Alley. The moon lights up a multitude of wild shrubs, trees, creeks, and flowers throughout 
the alley. This place acts as a hallway connecting the rest of campus. Your eye catches a glint of light 
reflecting off the moon and onto a shiny object. You follow the little light to a tin cap of a mason jar. 
The mason jar wedged within two branches of a sapling magnolia. You open the mason jar and realise there 
is an aged parchment map.
