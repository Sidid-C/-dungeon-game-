It is the 3rd Thursday night ever at college. You are bored and cannot sleep so you go for a walk 
around the domain, and stumble upon a beautiful abandoned house. As you approach the house it is 
engulfed in vines, and the ethereal stained glass windows sitting in the walls are cracked and shattered.
You peer inside one of the windows to see the inside. You rattle the door knob with no luck and finally 
reach through the shattered colorful glass and unlock the antique, red iron door. 
As the door swings open you walk into a building that is nice enough to be a church.
You are surrounded by mahogany wood everywhere, except for the solid concrete floor. 
There are letters carved into a wood panel just below a ginormous moose head hovering over a fireplace.
You blow away the dust to read A T O.  As you venture further into the house you reach the basement.
