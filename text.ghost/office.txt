The office is decorated with framed degrees and black and white photographs of 
old Sewanee football teams. A couple of oil portraits of old Vice Chancellors are
mounted to the wooden walls. Large velvet, purple curtains with gold tassel trimming 
hang from the ceiling complementing a stained glass chandelier above, and engulfing a 
large window below. Beside the window two bookshelves give the room symmetry. Centered
in the room is a huge polished wooden desk cluttered with the work of someone in the 
middle of a task. Behind the desk stands the person who saved you from the phantom… 
The Vice chancellor.
