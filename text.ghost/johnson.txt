You walk down the stone stairs that lead into Johnson, the metal hand rail chills 
your palm as your hand glazes over it for balance. The slightly curved, pebelled concrete 
walkway takes you all the way to the door, and under trees looming over you, as if they 
are ready to pounce. The door is already cracked open drawing you in. You push open the 
door and the light above buzzes and flickers, lighting up the commercial looking space. 
You take an abrupt left and enter the staircase. As you walk up the stairs someone pushes 
you down and you flip and roll down to the first floor. Shocked, terrified, and in pain 
you peer up into the gap in the middle of the staircase. You can see nothing, and hear 
nothing, but the flickering light and its light buzzing. You decide to try again, and you 
make it to the third floor. You are just beginning to look around when you are sent flying
 down the staircase, this time in between the gap straight to the first floor once again. 
 You are now battered and bruised, so you look to your left and see a common room.
