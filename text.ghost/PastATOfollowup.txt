The walls are covered with years of framed fraternity brothers. 
Their eyes seem to follow you as you wander around the room. 
There is a huge ornate wooden dresser in the corner littered with trash. 
A cream colored phone hangs off the side of the wall with a spiraling, 
spring cord attached to it. The dresser stands atop a massive patterned red rug that crawls 
underneath a semi circle of couches. Pleather and cloth couches are stacked on top of each 
other surrounding the stone fire pit. In the middle a stout coffee table that matches 
the dresser stands reinforced for dancing, with a newspaper clipping on top. The concrete 
floors are washed in a layer of mud. Through the window you can see beautiful greenery, 
and steeping stones leading down to abbot's alley.
