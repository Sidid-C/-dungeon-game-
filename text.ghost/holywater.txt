You notice the organ is playing, but when you look up to see who is playing, the music 
abruptly stops. You look back to the church admiring flowers that adorn the altar. Then 
the organ starts up again. You quickly whip your head around to see who is messing with 
you, but no one is there. The keys on the organ begin to frantically move up and down in 
forte; you can tell the song is reaching its climax. The keys begin to fly past you. As 
you dodge the ivory tiles the organ continues to play. A cold gust drives right through 
your chest, pushing you to fold over the baptismal font, and dip into the holy water. You 
rip your head from the font and spray the water all over the wooden chairs, as you run 
through the central aisle and out the front door. The door slams behind you, and the 
organist continues to play.
