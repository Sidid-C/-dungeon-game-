This is no ordinary rifle though, as you take a closer look into the cartridge, 
you realise you cannot load it with bullets. An inscription right next to the cartridge 
reads Aqua Sancta latin for holy water. 

You turn back to the door and As you are leaving you see an older woman standing on the 
stairs. Her sweet smile and wave disarm your fears for a moment. You wave back and make 
your way to the front door from which you came in. Suddenly, you feel a rough kick from 
the back and you are out the door quicker than expected. You turn to find the culprit’s 
sweet smile and wave through the window of the front door. The old woman quickly winks and 
vanishes into the recesses of Johnson to wait for another unsuspecting student to walk up 
those stairs.
