You run through branches and leaves as you search for the hidden trampoline in the woods. Spiderwebs connect the trees and act as 
obstacles in your path. It is dark in these woods and the sticky thin residue of the webs clings to your face and mouth. You spit 
out the web and grab at your face in a panic. Finally, you have cleaned the webs from your face and with your vision clear you can 
feel something watching you. You hear a yelp in the distance of a woman, it sounds like she's calling for help. You follow the sound 
of the whimpering woman. You catch a pair of bright yellow eyes watching you, and understand there was no woman at all. Slowly, you back 
away from the eyes that have not blinked yet. The creature slowly climbs down from the perch it was watching from to get a closer look at 
you. It has gray skin with little fur, and resembles a dog. But something is off. The dog-like creature begins to circle you.
