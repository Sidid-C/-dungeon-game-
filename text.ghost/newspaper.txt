The Sewanee Purple (Newspaper) 
		You glaze over the sports, weather and a couple of advertisement columns, 
        but what really catches your eye is the headliner, “ Vice Chancellor: A man with the Plan”. 
        The main article goes into great detail about the boundless wisdom of the Vice Chancellor. 
        It claims that the Vice chancellor knows the domain like the back of his hand, including 
        every way in and out. Additionally, the paper mentions the rumors about the chancellor’s 
        possible psychic connections to Sewanee. You take note of the Vice Chancellor's importance.
