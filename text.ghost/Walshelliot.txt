As you walk to Walsh-Ellot a chill goes through your spine and rattles your core. 
The wind pushes acorns out of the trees and they fall like hail all around you. 
A squirrel leaps from one tree to another making your head jerk to the left only 
to catch the swaying trees. You feel your heart hammering against your chest, but 
you continue on in the hopes of finding the Vice Chancellor in order to find your 
way back to 2026.
